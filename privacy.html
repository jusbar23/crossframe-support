Privacy policy

Short version: your video stays on your device, and we don't collect personal information.

What we collect

Crossframe does not collect, store, or transmit personal information. We have no accounts, no login, and no analytics on what you record.

Your videos

Recordings are processed on your device and saved to your photo library. They are never uploaded to us or to any third party. We cannot see, access, or recover your footage.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera, to record video.
  • Microphone, to record audio with your video.
  • Photo library, to save finished recordings.

Each permission is used only for the purpose described, and you can revoke any of them in the iOS Settings app at any time.

Purchases

If you buy anything in the app, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple. We receive aggregate sales reports from Apple, which do not identify individual customers.

Children

Crossframe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to support@crossframe.app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date below.
